Retouch & tints

Foils
½ head for a touch of light, ¾ head to frame the face, or a full head for depth and texture.
Colour correction
Brassy tones or an uneven dye job, corrected to an even result you'll love.

Hair extensions

100% human hair, Russian tape, a natural blend.
Russian tape
UV- and water-resistant, so the pool and the beach are fine. Applied without heat, and it can last up to eight weeks.
Matched to you
We help you choose the set for the volume and length you're after, with aftercare advice to keep it looking its best.
