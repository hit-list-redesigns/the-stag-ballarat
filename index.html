Good questions.

Short answers to what people ask us most. For anything else, ring the salon.

How often should I get a cut?

Generally every four to six weeks keeps your shape and style. It depends on your lifestyle and hair type, though. Longer cuts and coarse or curly hair may need a different rhythm.

What is Russian tape?

A human-hair extension joined with a special adhesive tape. It goes on quickly and without heat, looks natural, and can last up to eight weeks.

What's balayage?

Colour painted onto sections of hair for a soft, sun-kissed, gradual effect. It looks more natural than traditional highlights and can be subtle or multi-tonal.

Highlights or lowlights?

Highlights lighten sections for a brightening effect. Lowlights darken sections with semi-permanent colour for a more dramatic, dimensional look. Both add depth and contrast.

What's ombre?

Colour where the roots are darker than the ends. It can be subtle or dramatic, and it adds dimension and contrast.
